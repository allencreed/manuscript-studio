# PART 1 — EXECUTIVE VISION & PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS

---

# 1. Executive Summary

## Product Name

**Hermes**

## Product Category

AI Manuscript Intelligence Studio

---

## Vision Statement

Hermes is an AI-powered desktop writing environment designed to transform traditional manuscripts into intelligent, interactive creative systems.

Unlike traditional writing software that only stores text, Hermes understands:

* characters
* scenes
* relationships
* timelines
* themes
* revisions
* author preferences

Hermes acts as an intelligent creative partner that helps authors write, edit, organize, analyze, and prepare manuscripts for publishing.

---

# 2. The Problem Hermes Solves

Modern authors struggle with:

## Manuscript Complexity

Large manuscripts contain thousands of interconnected elements:

* characters
* events
* locations
* motivations
* themes
* narrative arcs

Traditional editors and word processors treat these as disconnected text.

---

## Revision Challenges

Authors often lose track of:

* why changes were made
* previous versions
* character consistency
* timeline errors
* repeated ideas

---

## AI Limitations

Most AI writing tools:

* only see the current prompt
* lack manuscript memory
* do not understand story structure
* do not preserve author ownership

---

# 5. Core Product Principles

---

# Principle 1: Local First

Hermes protects creative ownership.

Default:

* local storage
* local AI processing
* encrypted projects

Cloud services are optional.

---

# Principle 2: Author Control

Hermes assists the author.

Hermes does not:

* replace creativity
* overwrite writing automatically
* force AI suggestions

The author remains the final decision maker.
